How the Virtual Balloon Works with Live Wind

Aerostar is a free virtual hot air balloon flight simulator powered by real-time wind data. Click anywhere on the map, press START, and watch your balloon drift according to actual meteorological conditions — wind speed, direction, and gusts — at that exact location anywhere on Earth.

Real Wind Simulation Technology

The balloon responds to live wind data including speed (measured in meters per second), direction in degrees, and local gusts. Unlike most simulators that use random or pre-programmed wind patterns, Aerostar fetches current weather conditions from global meteorological sources. The simulation covers the entire planet — from mountain thermals in the Swiss Alps to consistent ocean breezes across the Pacific.

Free and No Registration Required

You can fly as a guest immediately — the balloon will drift until you close your browser. For longer flights that persist across browser sessions and days of drifting, you can create a free account. No credit card required. No hidden fees. No ads. No trackers.

Why Real Wind Matters

Traditional balloon simulators use fake or predictable wind. Real wind creates authentic, unpredictable journeys. A launch over the Rocky Mountains behaves differently than a launch above the Amazon rainforest. You might drift into unexpected weather patterns, experience calm mornings with gentle breezes, or get carried by stronger afternoon gusts. Every flight is unique.

Technical Details

Wind data updates regularly from real-time meteorological APIs. The balloon's altitude is simulated at approximately 500 meters above ground level — where wind patterns differ significantly from surface conditions. Coordinates update every few seconds as the balloon drifts. The simulation is purely for relaxation and exploration — no competitive elements, no scores, no timers.
