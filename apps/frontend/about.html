About Aerostar

Aerostar is a virtual hot air balloon flight simulator powered by real-time wind data. Click anywhere on the map, press START, and watch your balloon drift according to actual meteorological conditions — wind speed, direction, and gusts — at that exact location.

🌬️ Live Wind Data

No simulated or random wind. The balloon responds to real weather conditions from global meteorological sources.

🌍 Global Coverage

Launch from any coordinates — Alps, Pacific Ocean, Sahara, Arctic Circle. Wind patterns vary by location.

🎈 Free & No Ads

Zero pop-ups, zero trackers, zero paywalls. Just a balloon and the wind. Supported by voluntary donations.

🔐 Optional Registration

Fly as a guest (balloon resets when browser closes) or register to keep your flight going for days.

How the Wind Simulation Works

The balloon's movement is calculated using live wind speed (m/s) and direction (degrees). Wind data updates regularly from real-time meteorological sources. The balloon's altitude is simulated at approximately 500 meters above ground level — where wind patterns differ from surface conditions.

Each time you choose a new starting point, the system fetches current wind conditions for that latitude and longitude. As the balloon drifts, wind data continuously updates, creating an authentic, unpredictable journey.

Why I Built This

Aerostar started as a personal experiment in "slow web" — a calm, meditative space away from notifications and algorithms. There's something peaceful about watching a balloon drift over mountains or oceans, guided only by the wind. No goals. No scores. Just flight.

The project runs entirely on server donations. No corporate funding, no ads, no data selling. Just people who enjoy virtual drifting.

Technical Notes

  • Wind source: Real-time meteorological data
  • Map: Leaflet + ESRI Satellite / OSM Standard
  • Coordinates update: Every few seconds during flight
  • Guest flights: Temporary, stored in browser session
  • Registered flights: Persistent across sessions
