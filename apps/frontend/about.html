About Aerostar

Aerostar is a web-based, real-time hot air balloon flight simulator designed for relaxation and slow exploration. The concept is simple: you launch a virtual balloon from any point on Earth, and it drifts across the globe using live, real-time meteorological wind data. It's a completely ad-free, zen browser experience created for anyone who loves geography, maps, and peaceful journeys.

🌬️ Real-Time Wind Data

Your flight path is determined entirely by current global weather conditions. No simulated or random wind.

🗺️ Global Map Exploration

Launch your balloon anywhere in the world — Alps, Pacific Ocean, Sahara, Arctic Circle — and watch where the wind takes you.

🧘 Zen & Meditative Experience

No points, no stress, no timers. Just pure, slow-living exploration from the safety of your screen.

🎈 Free & No Ads

Zero pop-ups, zero trackers, zero paywalls. Just a balloon and the wind. Supported by voluntary donations.

How the Wind Simulation Works

The balloon's movement is calculated using live wind speed (m/s) and direction (degrees). Wind data updates regularly from real-time meteorological sources. The balloon's altitude is simulated at approximately 500 meters above ground level — where wind patterns differ from surface conditions. Each time you choose a new starting point, the system fetches current wind conditions for that exact location, creating an authentic, unpredictable journey.

Why I Built This

Aerostar started as a personal experiment in "slow web" — a calm, meditative space away from notifications and algorithms. There's something peaceful about watching a balloon drift over mountains or oceans, guided only by the wind. No goals. No scores. Just flight.

Technical Notes

  • Wind source: Real-time meteorological data
  • Map: Leaflet + ESRI Satellite / OSM Standard
  • Coordinates update: Every few seconds during flight
  • Guest flights: Temporary, stored in browser session
  • Registered flights: Persistent across sessions
