Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 13, 2025

Data Collection

Aerostar collects minimal data:

  • Guest users: No personal data collected. Balloon position is stored in your browser's session storage and deleted when you close the tab.
  • Registered users: Email address and hashed password (stored securely). Used only for authentication and saving persistent balloon flights.
  • Wind data requests: Your approximate coordinates (latitude/longitude) are sent to weather APIs to fetch live wind data. These APIs may log requests temporarily.

No Trackers or Analytics

This website does not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any third-party tracking scripts. No cookies are used for advertising or profiling.

Third-Party Services

  • Map tiles: ESRI Satellite and OpenStreetMap — their privacy policies apply for map image loading.
  • Weather API: Wind data from a third-party meteorological provider.
  • Authentication: Email/password stored via backend (no OAuth providers).
  • Patreon: If you click the Patreon link, their privacy policy applies.

Your Rights

You can request deletion of your account and associated email at any time by contacting the project maintainer.

Contact

For privacy questions: admin@aerost.art
